Our Heritage

Born From a Singular Promise: What You Cherish Deserves to Outlast Time.

Founded in 2004 within New York’s historic Arts District, Frame & Preserve began as an intimate conservation bench for collectors, fine galleries, and archival preservationists. While mass manufacturing flooded the market with synthetic laminates and acidic paper mounts, we chose the slower, honorable path of the traditional framing guild.

Every frame that departs our studio is shaped by hand using sustainably harvested hardwoods—white oak, walnut, maple, and cherry—joined with hand-planed splines and finished with natural beeswax, micro-crystalline oils, or 22-karat genuine gold leaf.

We view custom framing not merely as an aesthetic enclosure, but as an environmental vault. By marrying time-tested joinery with optical-grade UV filtration and micro-climate sealing, we guarantee your work remains as radiant for future generations as it is today.

Conservation Philosophy

The Pillars of Museum-Grade Protection

True preservation demands unyielding standards. We adhere strictly to the conservation guidelines established by international museum conservation institutes.

100% Pure Rag Archival Mats

We never use lignin or wood-pulp based matboards. Our mats are crafted exclusively from naturally acid-free, buffered 100% cotton fibers that will never yellow, bleed chemicals, or harm delicate paper emulsions.

  • ✓ Certified acid-free & lignin-free
  • ✓ Solid color core & archival buffering

Optically Pure Museum Glazing

Harmful ultraviolet frequencies degrade organic pigments within months. Our anti-reflective museum glass and acrylics filter out up to 99% of harmful UV rays while transmitting light with crystal clarity and virtually zero glare.

  • ✓ 99% UV-blocking optical filtration
  • ✓ Magnetron sputtered anti-reflection

Reversible Artisan Hinging

The cornerstone of museum conservation: any mounting technique must be 100% reversible without trace. We utilize pure Japanese Kozo mulberry paper and archival wheat-starch paste cooked fresh in our atelier.

  • ✓ Zero pressure-sensitive adhesive tapes
  • ✓ Natural expansion & contraction allowance
Method & Precision

The Atelier Journey

Every artwork undergoes an exhaustive, four-stage journey from initial optical inspection to white-glove encapsulation.

01

Curatorial Consultation

We analyze piece medium, tonal balance, paper condition, and display lighting to specify matching proportions and wood profiles.

02

Hand-Milled Joinery

Mouldings are miter-cut on double-blade precision saws, planed for true flush joints, and reinforced with internal hardwood splines.

03

Archival Encapsulation

Art is hinged using pure Japanese Kozo paper. Spacers ensure glazing never touches the artwork surface, preventing moisture adhesion.

04

Sealing & Installation

An archival moisture barrier dust-cover is thermal-sealed. Heavy-gauge d-rings, stainless wire, and silicone wall bumpers are affixed.
