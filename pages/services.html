Bespoke Guild Framing

Custom Framing Atelier

Custom picture framing at Frame & Preserve is a collaborative curatorial process. We do not use generic, mass-produced pre-cut mouldings. Every single commission begins with an individualized structural assessment of your artwork's medium, paper weight, and aesthetic balance to design an enclosure that enhances without overwhelming.

Architectural Mouldings & Hardwood Selection

Choose from over 450 distinct moulding profiles milled from sustainably harvested North American hardwoods—including American Black Walnut, Rift-Sawn White Oak, Rock Maple, and Cherry. Mouldings are precision miter-cut on double-blade saws, trued with hand planes, and permanently reinforced with internal hardwood splines rather than brittle metal V-nails.

Archival Cotton Matting & Curatorial Borders

We utilize 100% pure cotton rag matboards (Crescent / Bainbridge Museum Rag) that are completely acid-free and buffered with calcium carbonate to actively neutralize environmental acidity. Available in single, double, and 8-ply extra-thick bevels, as well as hand-wrapped Japanese raw silk and archival linen liners.

Optically Pure Museum Glazing

We equip pieces with Tru Vue Museum Glass featuring 99% UV radiation absorption and magnetron-sputtered anti-reflection (<1% visible reflection). For oversized and transit installations, we provide Optium Museum Acrylic—shatter-resistant, anti-static, and optical-grade.

Conservation Science

Art Preservation Standards

Preservation is not passive storage; it is active environmental defense. Organic art substrates—such as watercolor on cotton, charcoal on rag, antique documents, and oil pastels—continually interact with temperature, humidity, airborne pollutants, and photochemical light energy. Our certified conservation protocols halt degradation permanently.

100% Reversible Mulberry Paper Hinging

In strict compliance with International Institute for Conservation (IIC) tenets, no non-reversible adhesive ever touches your art. We mount using hand-torn Japanese Kozo mulberry paper adhered with pure, gluten-purified wheat starch paste, easily released with distilled water without leaving trace marks or residue.

Micro-Climate Vapor & Dust Barrier Sealing

Our frames feature archival metalized film vapour barriers, closed-cell foam backing, and impermeable conservation dust covers. This isolates the inner package from moisture swings, household humidity surges, airborne greases, and insect intrusions.

Optical Separation & Glazing Spacers

Glazing must never contact the art surface directly, as temperature condensation causes pigments to adhere permanently to glass. We incorporate hidden archival acrylic spacers or deep mat windows to maintain a safe protective air chamber.

Dimensional & Unique Pieces

Specialty Framing Solutions

Beyond flat works on paper, our workshop engineers custom structural enclosures for textiles, canvas floaters, sports memorabilia, and 3D architectural relics.

01. Original Art

Canvas Floaters

Custom hardwood floating frames designed specifically for gallery-wrapped oils and acrylics. A recessed 3/8-inch shadow channel creates the illusion that the painted canvas is levitating within the hardwood surround without obscuring the canvas edges.

Solid Walnut / Oak / Maple
02. Sports & Textiles

Athletic Jerseys

Hand-formed archival garment forms padded with acid-free batting to eliminate fabric creases. The textile is secured with hand-stitched invisible cotton threads—never glues or harmful staple guns—preserving the original jerseys for decades.

Hand-Stitched Non-Invasive
03. Documents & Blueprints

Parchments & Diplomas

Sheepskin vellum, diplomas, historic patents, and architectural blueprints demand specific tension-relieving mounts. We incorporate double 100% rag mats with hand-cut French lines and UV Museum Glass to prevent ink fading and paper cockling.

99% UV Barrier Included
04. Paper Ephemera

Historic Memorabilia

Concert stubs, vintage postcards, handwritten correspondence, and historical broadsides require encapsulated polyester (Melinex 516) archival mounts that hold fragile paper without any chemical adhesives touching the original item.

Zero Chemical Contact
05. 3D Architectural Boxes

Deep Shadow Boxes

Engineered with extra-deep rabbets from 2.5 to 6 inches, our shadow boxes feature fabric-wrapped inner sidewalls that eliminate glare bounce. Perfect for military medals, pocket watches, family heirloom silver, and sculpted reliefs.

Depths up to 6 Inches
06. Rare Antiquities

Antiquities & Objects

Custom armature mounts hand-forged in brass and powder-coated steel to support archaeological pottery, ceremonial daggers, and mineral specimens. Fully isolated with inert conservation silicone cushions.

Custom Brass Armatures
